Wallet size photo dimensions and how to print them from iPhone

By Mohamed Elatabany · Updated 8 November 2026

A wallet-size photo is usually 2 × 3 inches (5.08 × 7.62 cm). Some photo labs and frames use 2.5 × 3.5 inches (6.35 × 8.89 cm) instead, so measure the sleeve or frame before you print. To print wallets from an iPhone at their real size, give the photo that exact size in a layout app, put several on one sheet, and print at 100%.

A small gap makes each wallet easier to cut cleanly. No gap fits more photos, and neighbouring photos share one cut line. The preview in the app always shows the real count for your margin, gap and page orientation.

Print wallet photos from your iPhone

The Photos app prints to the paper size you pick. It has no 2 × 3 in setting and cannot arrange several wallets on one sheet. In Photo Print:

  1. Tap New Layout and choose Wallet (2 × 3 in), or Custom for 2.5 × 3.5 in or your frame’s size.
  2. Choose your paper: US Letter, A4, 4 × 6 or 5 × 7. Then add your photos.
  3. Open a photo and set Copies, or add different photos. Fill spare space previews how many extra copies still fit.
  4. Choose Fit or Fill for each photo and check faces near the edges of the crop.
  5. On the Page tab, set Page margin and Space between photos, and keep Cut guides on.
  6. Check the preview. A photo that does not fit is listed with the largest size that would, never quietly shrunk.
  7. Print with AirPrint, or save a PDF and print it at Actual Size / 100%. Measure one wallet with a ruler, then trim.

Four wallets from one 4 × 6 print

Four 2 × 3 in wallets fill a 4 × 6 in print exactly, in a two-by-two grid. That makes a photo lab useful when you have no printer: one 4 × 6 print gives you four wallets.

  1. Make the layout on 4 × 6 paper with the Wallet size.
  2. Set Space between photos to 0 on the Page tab. With the default 3 mm gap, only two fit.
  3. Give the photo 4 copies, or use four different photos.
  4. On the Export tab, choose No printer? Use a photo lab. The app saves a 4 × 6 in photo (1200 × 1800 pixels at 300 PPI) to share or save.
  5. Order it as a 4 × 6 print at a kiosk or online lab. If the order screen offers a choice, pick the option that does not crop or enlarge the photo, then cut along the grid.

A home printer that cannot print to the paper edge will clip this layout. Use Letter or A4 instead, or follow the photo-lab guide for more on ordering.

Fit or Fill?

Choose based on what matters in the photo
ModeResultUse it when
FitKeeps the whole image; blank space can remain inside the wallet rectangle.You need to keep everyone or an important detail.
FillCovers the rectangle; some of the original image is cropped.You want an edge-to-edge wallet and can adjust the crop.

An iPhone camera photo is usually 3:4, and a 2 × 3 in wallet is 2:3, so Fill trims a little from the sides of an upright photo. Drag and zoom the crop to keep heads and hands inside.

Keep small prints sharp

A 2 × 3 in wallet needs 600 × 900 visible pixels for 300 PPI. A heavily zoomed crop uses fewer of the original pixels. Check the app’s quality rating after adjusting the crop, and use the original photo rather than a compressed copy from a messaging app. The photo size calculator works out pixel targets for any size.

Frequently asked questions

What size is a wallet photo?

Usually 2 × 3 inches (5.08 × 7.62 cm). Some photo labs and frames use 2.5 × 3.5 inches (6.35 × 8.89 cm). Measure the sleeve, frame or card the photo is for, and print that size.

How many wallet photos fit on a 4 × 6 print?

Four 2 × 3 inch wallets fill a 4 × 6 inch print exactly, with no gaps and no margin, so they need a borderless print or a photo lab. With 3 mm gaps between them, two fit. Only two 2.5 × 3.5 inch wallets fit on a 4 × 6.

How many wallet photos fit on a Letter or A4 page?

With a ¼ inch page margin and 3 mm gaps, ten 2 × 3 inch wallets fit on US Letter or A4. Without gaps, thirteen fit on Letter and eleven on A4. For 2.5 × 3.5 inch wallets: eight on Letter and nine on A4.

How many pixels does a wallet photo need?

For 300 PPI, a 2 × 3 inch print needs 600 × 900 pixels and a 2.5 × 3.5 inch print needs 750 × 1050 pixels, counted after cropping. A normal iPhone camera photo has far more than that.

Can I print wallet photos from my iPhone without an app?

The Photos app prints to the paper size you choose; it has no 2 × 3 inch setting and cannot arrange several wallets on one sheet. Use a layout app such as Photo Print, or order wallet prints from a photo lab that sells them.
