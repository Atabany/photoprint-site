Printing at the right size

  • Printing from the app uses AirPrint at true size. Pick the same paper in the printer options as in the layout.
  • If you print a saved PDF from another app or a computer, choose 100% or Actual Size and turn off Fit to Page.
  • Most printers can't print within about 5 mm of the paper's edge; keep the page margin at least that unless your printer prints borderless.
