# photoprint-site

The public pages for **Photo Print: Size & Layout**.

- `privacy.html` — App Store Connect *Privacy Policy URL*
- `support.html` — App Store Connect *Support URL*
- `guides.html` and the four how-to pages it lists — printing guides (passport/ID photos,
  exact-size 4 × 6, several photos on one page, wrong-size prints)
- `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` — all crawlers allowed, including GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User

`privacy.html` and `support.html` were generated by `python3 tools/build_site.py <this-dir>` in the
app repo, and the privacy policy's source is `docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md` there. The guides, the
sitemap, the Guides nav link, `<main>` landmarks, canonical links and the darker light-mode accent
(`#ad4f2b`, for WCAG AA contrast on the card background) were added here by hand; **re-running
`build_site.py` as it stands would drop them from `index.html`, `support.html` and `privacy.html`**
— port them into the generator first.

No cookies, no analytics, no external requests. (The JSON-LD blocks are structured data, not scripts.)
