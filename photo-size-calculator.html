Photo size calculator: pixels, inches, cm and mm

By Mohamed Elatabany · Updated 8 November 2026

One inch is exactly 25.4 mm. To find the pixels a print needs, multiply each side in inches by the PPI (300 for sharp photo prints). To find how large you can print a photo, divide its pixel width and height by the PPI.

Size to pixels: what a print needs

2 × 2 in = 50.8 × 50.8 mm = 5.08 × 5.08 cm
At 300 PPI: at least 600 × 600 visible pixels.

Calculated in your browser. No photo upload. Pixel targets describe resolution after cropping, not identity-document compliance.

Pixels to size: how big can I print this photo?

4032 × 3024 pixels at 300 PPI: up to 13.44 × 10.08 in (34.14 × 25.6 cm)
Larger prints are possible at a lower PPI, with less fine detail.

On iPhone, swipe up on a photo in the Photos app (or tap the info button) to see its pixel dimensions. Use the size after cropping.

How big can you print an iPhone photo?

Recent iPhones save camera photos at 12, 24 or 48 megapixels, depending on the model and camera settings. These are the largest prints each size gives at full frame, without cropping.

Use the original units for exact printing

The table rounds decimal inches for readability. For a 35 × 45 mm print, enter 35 and 45 in millimetres in the app instead of entering rounded inch values. For a 2 × 2 inch photo, enter inches: 50 × 50 mm is a smaller rectangle.

How to calculate print pixels

For a 4 × 6 inch print at 300 PPI, multiply 4 × 300 and 6 × 300: 1200 × 1800 pixels. For 35 × 45 mm, divide by 25.4 first and multiply by 300: approximately 413.39 × 531.50, rounded up to 414 × 532 pixels.

How to calculate the largest print size

Divide each pixel dimension by the PPI. A 4032 × 3024 photo at 300 PPI: 4032 ÷ 300 = 13.44 in and 3024 ÷ 300 = 10.08 in. Multiply inches by 2.54 for centimetres: 34.1 × 25.6 cm. If you crop the photo first, use the pixel size of the cropped part.

Pixel dimensions do not decide the printed size

The same file can be printed at several physical sizes. Its PPI changes with the chosen dimensions and crop. Photo Print lets you set physical dimensions directly and check quality in the finished layout. See pixels, PPI and cropping.
