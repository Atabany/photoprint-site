Photo Print — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Photo Print: Size & Layout ("Photo Print", "the app") is made by Mohamed Elatabany. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your photos stay on your device, and there is no account.

Photos

When you add photos to a layout, the app copies them from the system photo picker into its own private storage on your device, so that saved layouts open again later. The app never uploads photos anywhere, never reads your photo library on its own (the system picker only hands over what you choose), and deletes its copies when you delete the layout.

PDFs you save or share, and print jobs you send, go where you send them: to the location you choose in Files, to the app you share to, or to the printer you pick through AirPrint.

Purchases

The Pro unlock is a one-time purchase through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple's privacy policy. The app uses RevenueCat to verify the purchase and restore it on your other devices; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and purchase receipt data, and nothing about your photos or layouts. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Other apps

Settings has a “More apps” section listing the developer’s other apps. When you open Settings, the app asks Apple’s public App Store lookup service for those apps’ current names, taglines and icons, so the list is never out of date. The request contains the developer’s Apple ID and your App Store region; it contains nothing about you, your device or your photos, and the answer is a public App Store listing. The result is kept in the app’s cache for a week so the section works offline. Tapping an app opens the App Store.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or personal profile.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No server of its own. The only network traffic is the App Store purchase and its verification, and the App Store listing request described above.

Data on your device

Layouts, their photos, and your preferences (measurement unit, how many free exports you have used) are stored in the app's own container on the device and are included in your device backups. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the Support page linked from the app's Settings.
