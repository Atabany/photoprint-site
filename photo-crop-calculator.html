Why a 4 × 6 crops an iPhone photo

Shape is width divided by height. A 4 × 6 print is 6 ÷ 4 = 1.5; an iPhone photo is 4032 ÷ 3024 ≈ 1.33. To make the photo as long as the print, it keeps its full 4032 pixel length and only 4032 ÷ 1.5 = 2688 of its 3024 pixel width. The other 336 rows, 11.1% of the picture, are cut, half from each side.

Which edges get cut

If the print is longer and narrower than the photo, as a 4 × 6 is for an iPhone photo, the crop shortens the photo's short side: it cuts the top and bottom of a landscape shot, or the left and right of a portrait one. If the print is squarer, as an 8 × 10 is, the crop shortens the long side instead: the left and right of a landscape shot. A square print always shortens the long side.

Keep heads and edges in the picture

  • Move the crop. In Photo Print, open a photo's crop and drag or zoom so the important part is inside the frame.
  • Fit instead of fill. The whole photo prints, with blank paper on two sides.
  • Pick a matching size. 6 × 8 or 9 × 12 in for iPhone photos; 4 × 6 or 8 × 12 in for 3:2 camera photos.

ID photos crop differently because they are always portrait. A portrait iPhone photo loses only about 4% to a 35 × 45 mm passport photo and 25% to a square 2 × 2 in one, so leave space around the head when you take it. See 2 × 2 photos and 35 × 45 mm photos.

Frequently asked questions

Why does a 4 × 6 print cut off part of my iPhone photo?

An iPhone photo is 4:3 and a 4 × 6 print is 3:2, which is longer and narrower. To cover the paper, about 11% of the photo is cut away: the top and bottom of a landscape photo, or the sides of a portrait one.

Which print size fits an iPhone photo without cropping?

Sizes in the 4:3 shape: 3 × 4, 6 × 8, 9 × 12 or 12 × 16 in. On 4 × 6 paper you can print a 4 × 5.33 in photo and trim the extra strip. Photo Print accepts any custom size in inches, cm or mm.

Is 8 × 10 the same shape as 4 × 6?

No. 8 × 10 is 4:5, close to square, so it shortens a photo’s long side. A 3:2 camera photo loses about 17% to an 8 × 10, but nothing to a 4 × 6.

Is A4 the same shape as US Letter?

No. A4 is 1 : 1.414 and Letter is 1 : 1.294, so a photo filling A4 is cropped differently from one filling Letter.

Can I choose which part of the photo is kept?

Yes. The calculator shows a centred crop, which is what most labs and apps start from. In Photo Print you can move and zoom the crop for each photo, or switch to Fit to keep the whole picture.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Your browser opens the photo on your device to draw the previews. Nothing is sent anywhere.
