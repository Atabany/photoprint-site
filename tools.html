Free photo printing tools

By Mohamed Elatabany · Updated 15 November 2026

Six free tools for getting a photo print right the first time. They run in your browser, need no account and never upload your photos. The sheet and quality maths follows the same rules as the Photo Print app.

Photos per page calculatorHow many 2 × 2, passport, wallet or 4 × 6 photos fit on 4 × 6, 5 × 7, A4 or Letter, with a to-scale preview. Print quality checkerChoose a photo and a print size: see the PPI, whether it will be sharp, and the largest good size. Crop calculatorSee what a 4 × 6, 5 × 7, 8 × 10, wallet, square or A4 print cuts off your photo. Photo size calculatorConvert inches, cm and mm, find the pixels a print needs, or how large a photo can print. Printer test sheetFree A4, Letter and 4 × 6 PDFs with rulers and a 50 mm square to check actual size and margins. Print scale helperMeasured the test sheet? Enter the lengths to see exactly how much your printer shrank or enlarged it.

Which tool do I need?

  • “How many passport photos fit on a 4 × 6?” Use the photos per page calculator.
  • “Is this photo good enough to print at 8 × 10?” Use the print quality checker.
  • “Why did my 4 × 6 cut off someone's head?” Use the crop calculator.
  • “How many pixels is 35 × 45 mm?” Use the photo size calculator.
  • “My prints come out smaller than they should.” Print the printer test sheet, then read why prints come out the wrong size.

How these tools work

Every tool runs in your browser. Photos you choose are opened on your device to read their size and draw previews, and are never sent anywhere. There are no cookies, accounts or analytics on this site.

The photos-per-page, quality and crop tools use the layout and quality rules of the Photo Print app, checked against the app's own code, so a sheet planned here matches the sheet the app makes. The same rule applies everywhere: a photo is never shrunk to fit. If it is too big for the paper, the tool says so.
